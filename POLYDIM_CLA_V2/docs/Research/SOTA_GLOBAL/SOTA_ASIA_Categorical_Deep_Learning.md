# SOTA Asia 2025-2026: Categorical Deep Learning (CDL)

## 1. Overview of Regional Developments
In the 2025-2026 period, "Categorical Deep Learning" (CDL) has solidified as a theoretical framework to provide a formal, algebraic foundation for neural network architectures (e.g., Gavranović et al.). Rather than being an exclusive proprietary development of Chinese AI labs, CDL is an international effort where Chinese institutions are actively participating.

### Key Theoretical Contributions
- **Tsinghua University:** Researchers have actively engaged in international efforts like the "Structural AI" workshops (2025). Literature from Tsinghua (e.g., School of Software) references categorical methods, particularly applied to hypergraph neural networks and complex activity recognition.
- **DeepSeek & Qwen in CDL:** Interestingly, while DeepSeek and Qwen models are frequently cited in 2025-2026 CDL literature, they are primarily treated as state-of-the-art benchmarks or empirical systems whose behaviors (like RL-based reasoning) researchers are attempting to formalize through categorical lenses. The models themselves are empirical; CDL provides the algebraic theory to verify them.
- **Agentic AI & Scientific Discovery:** New categorical frameworks are being applied to agentic systems, allowing workflows to alter their own schema compositionally.

## 2. Mathematical Foundations
CDL utilizes the algebra of monads to create a unified language for specifying and verifying deep learning architectures. A primary focus is on "compositional guarantees"—ensuring that if individual modules are mathematically correct, their composition remains correct by construction, contrasting with the "black-box" nature of massive models like DeepSeek.

## 3. Evaluation against the POLYDIM Theoretical Framework
- **Alignment:** POLYDIM's multi-dimensional approach to latent spaces benefits from CDL's rigor. CDL can provide the formal proofs (e.g., using monads and coalgebras) for the dimensional transformations posited by POLYDIM.
- **Integration:** To fully leverage CDL, POLYDIM should map its latent multi-dimensional agents as categorical objects and their interactions as morphisms, allowing for provable stability (drawing from recent unifications of CDL with Lyapunov dynamics).
